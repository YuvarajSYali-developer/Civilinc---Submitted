How It Works

From filing to field — tracked at every step

01
Department logs a project or complaint
Officers submit project proposals or citizen grievances through the portal. Each entry is automatically geo-tagged, timestamped, and assigned a priority level.
02
Smart routing to the right authority
The system routes each item to the correct department and officer based on ward, category, and current workload — with full audit trail from day one.
03
Live tracking visible to all stakeholders
Progress is visible on the city map in real time. Supervisors, coordinators, and commissioners see exactly what's active, what's delayed, and what needs escalation.
04
Resolution logged and data retained
Closed projects feed back into analytics. Historical data informs future budget allocations, resource planning, and governance reports — nothing is lost.

Security

Governance demands uncompromising security

CivilInc was architected from day one for the data sensitivity requirements of government infrastructure.

JWT-based Authentication
Every session is token-bound with short-lived access tokens and server-enforced expiry. No persistent sessions, no flat credential storage.
Granular RBAC
Permissions enforced at the API layer — not just the UI. Department isolation ensures no cross-ward data leakage at any access tier.
Full Audit Trail
Every read, write, and status change is immutably logged with user, timestamp, and IP. Governance audits pull structured reports in one click.
